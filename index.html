Mckay Anderson Assistant Line

The Mckay Anderson Assistant Line, (385) 220-8464, is a personal assistant text line run by Mckay Anderson, a sole proprietor. It texts only two individuals who are personally known to Mckay Anderson and who chose to opt in to SMS after he explained the program in person. They text the line first with a request, and the assistant replies to confirm the request and send status updates on it.

SMS consent is optional and not required to use the service. People who don’t opt in to SMS can get the same updates by email.

This line does not send marketing or promotional messages, and it does not text anyone who has not opted in.
