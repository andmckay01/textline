Privacy Policy

Mckay Anderson Assistant Line · (385) 220-8464
Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mckay Anderson Assistant Line (the “Assistant Line”) handles information. The Assistant Line is a personal assistant text line run by Mckay Anderson, a sole proprietor, and used only by two opted-in individuals who are personally known to Mckay Anderson. SMS consent is optional and not required to use the service.

Information we collect

We collect only the following from the two opted-in users:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The content of the text messages you send to the Assistant Line, and the replies it sends back.

We do not collect any other personal information through the Assistant Line.

How we use your information

Your phone number and message content are used only to receive and respond to your requests: to understand the task you asked for, to carry it out, and to text you updates about it. We do not use your information for marketing or advertising.

Sharing of information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are sent and received through our SMS service provider (Twilio) only so the messages can be delivered. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

Data retention

Message content and phone numbers are kept only as long as needed to respond to your requests and keep a short history of your tasks, and no longer than 12 months. If you opt out, or you ask us to delete your data, we delete your message history within 30 days, except where we must keep a record of your opt-out so we do not text you again.

How to opt out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to (385) 220-8464. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages after that. Reply HELP for help. You can also opt out by emailing the address below.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests to access or delete your data: andmckay01@gmail.com
